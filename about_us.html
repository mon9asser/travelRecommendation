About Wayfare

We're a small team of former guides and travel writers who got tired of generic top-ten lists.

Our story

Wayfare started in 2021 when three friends realized every trip they loved best came from a tip whispered by someone who actually lived there, not a search engine. We built Wayfare to be that friend: a place with a handful of genuinely great recommendations for beaches, temples, and cities, instead of a thousand mediocre ones.

Every listing on this site is researched and written by our own team, and we only add a place once someone on staff — or a trusted local contributor — has been there themselves.

What we do

We curate travel recommendations by region and theme, help you search them by keyword, and point you toward booking when you've found the trip you want. No ads, no sponsored placements — just the places worth your time.
